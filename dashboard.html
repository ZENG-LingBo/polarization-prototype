Cohorts — create, advance the day, close

Advancing the day re-seeds that day's discussion prompts. Participants join with the cohort code at study.html; on Days 2–3 they also enter the personal rejoin code issued on Day 1.

Operational go/no-go gates — computed from live data

Pre-registered run-time checks (Ray's G1–G3): they decide whether a day is ready to advance, and are not evidence for the hypothesis. A Day-1 baseline selected for being high regresses toward the mean on its own, so the reported effect is the arm × day contrast, never a within-arm drop.
